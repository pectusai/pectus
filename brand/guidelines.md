# Brand guidelines

This file is your long-form brand reference. Anything that doesn't fit the structured fields in `brand.json` lives here.

Skills read this file when the brand voice or tonality matters. The `weekly-analysis` skill, for example, considers it when judging whether a suggested article title is on-brand.

## Suggested sections

- **Voice** — how you sound. Long-form expansion of `brand.json#voice`.
- **Tonality** — how you adjust voice for different audiences and moments. The Teamtailor reference framework distinguishes calm/inspiring/different/human as the dimensions; adapt this to your own.
- **Vocabulary** — words you use, words you avoid. Important for skill outputs to feel like you wrote them.
- **Story** — what you're building, why now, who you're for.
- **Don'ts** — things that will never appear in your content.

Don't feel obligated to fill every section — leave anything blank that doesn't apply yet.
